## Geometry and perception

**Missing spoon body frame — highest geometry uncertainty.** The local roster
contains spoon episodes but no published spoon scan. Its reference body axes and
origin therefore cannot be inferred from a supplied mesh. The provisional
centroid/principal-axis rule requires a written convention review; its configured
review note remains null. A metric hull can still be wrong in the reference body
frame. Organiser clarification or an explicitly reviewed convention is needed;
a world alignment cannot generally remove body-frame error through motion.

**Calibration transfer.** Public calibration is assumed to apply to evaluation
recordings. The historical stereo report contains a small sampled vertical
residual; it does not establish its cause or full-dataset extent. Rig consistency
is an organiser question, while broader image-only geometry evidence would
require separately authorized work. No learned stereo or SLAM dependency is
implicitly approved as a remedy.

**Table normal as gravity.** The world assumes a level table, with no inertial
reference to establish it. A tilted plane or poor static texture can affect
camera tracking and simulator stability. Keep the recorded plane/origin rule,
landmark support and invalid-frame evidence visible. Do not hide a poor world
fit by large seating corrections.

**Identity, symmetry and occlusion.** Persistent masks can switch identities;
geometric registration can lose orientation around symmetric shapes; anonymous
hand mask slots can cross. Recovery searches and confidence heuristics are
implemented, but no full-video evidence exists. A high geometric fit score is
not proof of correct object identity or anatomical side.

**Unseen geometry.** Hulls fill cavities and TSDF surfaces can remain incomplete.
Thin spoon geometry may approach depth noise. Closedness for simulation and shape
accuracy for CD-O are different requirements. Mesh diagnostics compare only
available references; a missing spoon scan must remain unscorable, not zero error.

**Hand orientation and completion.** Pinned model integration, mirror conventions
and MANO frame mapping remain unexecuted. Selected-frame overlays cannot establish
metric depth or full-clip identity. Bone-preserving interpolation avoids one
algebraic collapse mode, but does not enforce joint limits, recover hidden motion
or guarantee clear parking transit.

**Contacts and collision.** Sixteen-link proximity uses sampled proxies; reported
penetration uses a limited joint-based diagnostic. Neither is an exact whole-hand
collision model. Closed, consistently oriented collision assets and correct
masses/supports remain necessary for meaningful simulator behavior.

## Contract questions

**Q06 — Cross-episode mesh reuse.** The project currently requires each submission
mesh to be reconstructed from its own episode. Reuse is not permitted merely
because two episodes name the same object. If organisers explicitly permit it,
record the authorization and source split/episode/frame lineage before changing
the contract. See [object mesh](../../track3/features/object_mesh/README.md).

Other unresolved competition questions include evaluation-rig calibration,
permitted stereo use, spoon frame/reference assets, evaluator allowlists and
remote quota/reset details. The [contract report](../../reports/contract.md)
contains the dated local source reading. Current external policies were not
rechecked for this documentation task. Before a future authorized release,
resolve time-sensitive questions against authoritative current sources rather
than treating this register as a live rules page.

## Keeping the register useful

Close an item with a dated source decision or concrete evidence reference. Keep
scope explicit: one public episode cannot settle all evaluation episodes; one
frame cannot establish full-clip tracking; one successful allocation cannot
establish training memory fit. Record an unknown as unknown until the authorized
work supplies evidence. Link resulting implementation changes to the owning
feature and the [delivery ledger](version1.md).
