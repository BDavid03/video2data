### GPU was idle (user report): two root causes, both fixed

1. `GpuLease.require_headroom` spawned `nvidia-smi` (0.27 s on WSL) twice per optimizer step
   and the sampler polled every 0.5 s. Now it reads the sampler thread's samples (interval 2 s).
   `PreparedPairs.__getitem__` also re-hashed `dataset.json` and the producer `run.json` on
   every sample; it now hashes each sample once (arrays identical old vs new).
   UNet 30-epoch training: ~400 s -> 117 s.
2. Every `attention=True` student recipe diverged ("Too many non-finite gradient steps").
   Isolated to the fp32 memory-efficient SDPA backward kernel on this GPU (grad norm 178 vs
   6.5 with the MATH kernel, identical forward). `AttentionBottleneck` now forces
   `SDPBackend.MATH` and zero-initialises its residual projections. Iteration 23 (attention)
   then trained normally (composite 0.530, fails the bias gate).
